# Glossary

**Admin** — A DocFit staff member who approves Doctor applications and manages the medicines database.
_Avoid:_ moderator, staff, team
_Related:_ Doctor

**Appointment** — A confirmed, paid booking of a Slot between a Patient and a Doctor. An Appointment exists only after payment is confirmed via SSLCommerz.
_Avoid:_ booking, reservation
_Related:_ Slot, Consultation

**Consultation** — The 30-minute video session between a Patient and a Doctor that takes place at the scheduled Appointment time.
_Avoid:_ session, call, meeting
_Related:_ Appointment, Prescription

**Doctor** — A medical professional who has registered on DocFit and been approved by an Admin to offer Consultations.
_Avoid:_ physician, provider, specialist (use Specialty to indicate their field)
_Related:_ Specialty, Slot, Prescription

**Patient** — A registered user who books Consultations on DocFit.
_Avoid:_ user, customer, client
_Related:_ Appointment, Prescription

**Prescription** — A structured medical document generated by a Doctor after a Consultation, containing diagnosis, medicines, and instructions.
_Avoid:_ prescription note, medical note
_Related:_ Consultation, Doctor

**Slot** — A 30-minute time block that a Doctor has marked as available on their schedule. A Slot becomes an Appointment when booked and paid.
_Avoid:_ time slot (redundant — use Slot), opening
_Related:_ Doctor, Appointment

**Specialty** — One of the five medical categories a Doctor belongs to and a Patient is routed to: Medicine, Orthopedics, Dermatology, Gynecology, Pediatrics.
_Avoid:_ department, category, domain
_Related:_ Doctor, Specialty Match

**Specialty Match** — The output of the AI model that maps a Patient's symptom description to one or more Specialties.
_Avoid:_ recommendation, prediction, diagnosis (DocFit does NOT diagnose)
_Related:_ Specialty, Patient
